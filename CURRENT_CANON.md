## How to read the campaign

- **PLAYED:** Events the DM reported occurring at the table.
- **ESTABLISHED:** Direct, explicit DM decisions.
- **PREPARED:** Approved premise for a future encounter, but not yet played.
- **OPEN:** Undecided. Do not turn earlier drafts into facts.

Played events and the DM's explicit later decisions prevail over older outlines. World inhabitants have independent motives, schedules and knowledge. Preparing the world's *DNA* thoroughly is an established part of how the DM runs; do not substitute thin scene scripts for table-ready prep.

## Bellweather now

The city's Council sold Bellweather to the Crown while the party was at Kellan's Well. The Council, **including Perrin Cardon**, left. The characters did not cause the sale.

**King Hendrix Stucky** is the monarch. **Prince Reginald Stucky**, governing with his wife **Princess Narcia Stucky**, administers Bellweather. The royalty is theatrically cruel and entitled. Crown government, clerks and guards remain consequential institutions.

**Koven House** is royal headquarters and registry. **Ledger House** is still independent and owned by **Brakkus Ledgerhelm**. **Bishop Brann** stayed behind with the city's people.

**Cardon Weavehouse belongs outright to the Crown.** The old name does not imply Perrin owns it or that there is an unresolved claim. **Jaroood** came with the prince and has **complete operational control** of the Crown-owned Weavehouse, including its personnel, worker assignments, discipline, procedures and debt administration. He believes debt-based slavery is justified, personally receives **10% of each additional debt assessed against each indentured servant**, fears the Crown and stays loyal because it makes him rich. He is a **243-year-old high elf**, 5 ft. 11 in. and slender, with a narrow face, pale gold eyes, and silver-blond hair combed back. He wears an immaculate burgundy coat, ivory waistcoat, and Crown signet ring. His voice is a **younger, more vigorous Mr. Burns**: nasal, refined, and condescending, never implying incompetence. He uses *Prestidigitation* to remove dust after factory inspections and has *Detect Magic* and *Misty Step* available through his high elf lineage. His daily routine is ledger review, inspections, petitions/fees, then closing accounts. His commission is Crown-authorized, not a hidden theft: on Elowen's quoted charges he earns **20 gp on the 200 gp fees, plus 5 gp per 50 gp daily charge**, no commission on the original 150 gp. Jaroood can sign a worker's release himself, and the current prepared site roster has three humanoid labor guards plus a separate Gargoyle, Mimic, and Earth Elemental protecting his private quarters. Refer to the full dossier and NPC stat block in the live JSON.

The Crown's control of old municipal service contracts, debt assessments and forced labor is real. Brann has proposed that the PCs appear to work for the Crown while covertly providing intelligence to him and Brakkus. The resistance is emerging, not a pre-established full conspiracy.

## Brakkus and the supernatural

Brakkus is a retired half-orc **Oath of Devotion paladin of Oghma**. His Hall of Heroes deity remains his NCFA deity. His mother **Elena**, a captive librarian, raised him to learn, read and avoid judging people by their ancestry; his father was the Ironfang warlord **Korgak**. Brakkus later learned from **High Justiciar Vance Halford**, who gave him the longsword **The Appendix**.

Brakkus **knows Aaron is a dhampir** and needs a conversation with him in Session 5. Brakkus implied **Oghma might have brought Aaron to Ledger House**, but divine intervention is *not* established.

Brakkus told the party a legend concerning possible mind-influencing magic in Bellweather. **DM ONLY:** the real magical phenomenon behind that legend is the **Radiant Well**, which exists **below the surface**. Brakkus and the party do not know its identity or location. The power's exact workings, entry route, and any royal knowledge or involvement are OPEN. The abandoned possessing-villain plot is **not active canon**.

## Played campaign history

**Sessions 1–3:** The party arrived amid a storm, established Ledger House as its base, explored the Slums and Blackstone Temple, and advanced to level 4 after Blackstone. At Blackstone it freed seven captives, killed Tal Rusk and remaining opposition, and returned the reliquary to Brann. The former Council imposed a five-mission service sentence on Pepper for Kaleb's killing: the Kellan's Well mission unpaid, four further missions paid.

**Kellan's Well:** The party stopped the alarm runner, used Fog Cloud to pass the Bow Gallery, discovered Noren through Aaron's reading of a Ranger's surface thoughts and Arunem's ceiling approach, then captured Noren and the Veteran. Noren warned of the impending sale. The Decanter of Endless Water went to Pepper. **Sluice restoration is not an outstanding task.**

**Session 4:** The party killed Noren and his Veteran. Arunem bagged the Veteran's decapitated body; the party met a guard who confirmed the Council was gone. Pepper shopped separately and had a bad interaction with **Edda Pell** of The Spare Button (now voiced with an **Irish accent**). Pepper reported Edda at Koven House, was recognized in royal contract records and detained for hard labor in the southern ruins. **Brakkus successfully negotiated her release into his custody.** Specific paperwork and custody terms are still OPEN.

Brann stated that he refused any Council-sale payout, presented the covert Crown-facing intelligence plan, and paid **100 gp each to Jarrock, Aaron and Arunem; 0 gp to Pepper**, the full outstanding Kellan's Well compensation. Those sums must not be paid again.

**Tulcornix Avemvir**, Kaleb's adopted aarakocra brother, sought help for Kaleb's widow **Elowen**, who is held at Cardon Weavehouse. At the table her claimed charges were **150 gp original debt + 200 gp fees + 50 gp per day room and board**. Pepper offered all her money. No payment or release has been reported.

**Session 5 launch:** The party has begun discussing a rescue. Aaron's familiar is **watching the Weavehouse overnight**; its observations have *not* been established. Brakkus recommends stealth to keep the planned covert role credible. Brakkus and Aaron's conversation remains to be played.

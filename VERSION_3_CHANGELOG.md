# No Cause for Alarm — Version 3.5

Version 3.5 is the current continuity-and-table-readiness baseline of **No Cause for Alarm: Ruins of Bellweather**. The playable campaign remains a four-character, level 3–16 5etools adventure. Locked map geometry, current rewards, published-stat-block policy, and the existing chapter order are preserved.

## Version 3 continuity foundation
- Restores **Doman** as the deceptive mage seeking the sealed planar vessel and locks **Commander Haren Vell** as the separate Iron Banner commander.
- Removes the abandoned **Tomas / Milton / missing notebook** thread.
- Locks **Sera Venn / Jorn Milar / 75 gp per character** as the opening Slums hooks.
- Uses **Independent Factions** instead of an undefined Regional Standing subsystem.
- Makes rescued **Reedscale** a minor supporting ally that can secure the safe river route without counting as one of Chapter 15's three strong alliances.
- Separates **Hessa Vale**, **Yaren**, and **Tyren**: Valen Cemetery and Maren Isle are independent threats, and Yaren does not know that Soren is Tyren's host.
- Defines **the Boss** as the apparent singular ruler presented by Tyren acting through a host; post-reveal DM text treats Tyren as the true intelligence.
- Adds a defined **Tyren Alert Track (0–3)** for Varen Keep.
- Removes obsolete **Milo Dorman** continuity.
- Renames **Tal Venn** to **Tal Rusk** to avoid falsely implying a relationship with Sera Venn.
- Standardizes **Radiant Well** terminology and removes obsolete uses of Pool.
- Makes **Perrin Cardon's political rise visible and institutional**, culminating in his becoming First Councilor before the Zennar betrayal.
- Adds **Rena Dask**, a small recurring labor subplot for Jarrock across 2–3 scenes.
- Preserves Chapter 15's advancement rule: **three strong alliances plus a safe route**.
- Preserves Aaron Dunamus's personal threads as optional, play-by-ear material separate from Tyren.

## Version 3.5 finishing pass
- Preserves the revised Session 1 social/combat flow, including the current brass-salvage scavenger presentation.
- Removes **Valen Cemetery** from the finale's Tyren-possession payoff chain so Hessa remains a genuinely independent threat.
- Standardizes post-Marren DM-facing terminology on **Tyren** while preserving **the Boss** in uninformed NPC speech, rumors, and in-world documents.
- Clarifies Marren Library's possession reveal: Tyren possesses the figure factions call the Boss and can move between hosts; the text no longer implies broad possession of the Boss's agents.
- Corrects the duplicated `specific specific` wording in encounter guidance.
- Adds a frozen **Version 3.5** recovery snapshot and updates repository documentation to match the live campaign.

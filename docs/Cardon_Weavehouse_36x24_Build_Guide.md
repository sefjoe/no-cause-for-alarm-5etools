## Terrain
Draw room walls and exterior boundaries on the mat. Place **8 STAHLHAMMERS door sections** at D1–D8 and **12 straight-wall sections** at W1–W12 as shown on the blue-and-orange [build overlay](../img/dm/cardon_weavehouse_36x24_build.svg). Real pieces are roughly 60 mm long, so align them locally with the drawn boundary without reducing corridor clearance. Physical door placements: D1 (21), D2 (23), D3 (19), D4 (9), D5 (10), D6 (17), D7 (18), D8 (11).

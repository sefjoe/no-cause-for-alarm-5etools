## Preserved existing bespoke entry

- Brakkus Ledgerhelm — existing custom full stat block retained unchanged.

## Intentionally not materialized

- Noncombat civic/story NPCs such as Bishop Brann, Wern, Council clerks, surveyors, caretakers, merchants, and community leaders.
- Unnamed encounter combatants and generic officers continue to use their published XMM entries.
- Perrin Cardon is not assigned a combat stat block because the current adventure presents his resolution as political exposure, bargaining, flight, or investigation rather than a required combat encounter.

## Notes

- Tyren, Manifested uses the Efreeti mechanics only for the optional manifested second phase already described by the finale.
- Jeren is materialized as a Mage because the finale describes him as a spellcasting advisor who may betray the party but does not provide a different published base.
- Balden is materialized as a Warrior Veteran because the Paden Square combat roster begins with one Veteran and Balden is the named security enforcer for that scene.
